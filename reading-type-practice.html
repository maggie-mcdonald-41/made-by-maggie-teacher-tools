How to Use the Grades 6–8 Reading Skills Trainers

What Students Can Practice

Built around common test-style reading questions:

  • Dedicated Grade-Level Content: Separate Grade 6, Grade 7, and Grade 8 passages, questions, standards, and benchmark assessments.
  • Multiple Passage Topics: Practice with engaging literary, informational, argumentative, historical, and paired texts.
  • Part A / Part B Questions: Answer layered questions that require analysis and supporting evidence.
  • Multiple Choice: Select the strongest answer from carefully designed, reasoning-based choices.
  • Multi-Answer Selection: Evaluate several choices and select every response supported by the text.
  • Sequencing: Drag and drop events, steps, or ideas into the correct order.
  • Sorting and Classification: Place details, claims, evidence, or ideas into the correct categories.
  • Matching: Connect related ideas, such as claims and evidence or terms and explanations.
  • Dropdown Questions: Complete sentences and analyses using embedded answer choices.
  • Highlight-to-Answer: Select exact text evidence directly from a passage.
  • Charts and Visual Sources: Analyze graphs, diagrams, images, and visual data.
  • Podcast and Video Questions: Practice listening, multimedia analysis, and integration of information across sources.
  • Linked Passage Auto-Flip: The trainer automatically opens the passage connected to the current question.
  • Practice and Benchmark Modes: Build skills during practice sessions and measure standards mastery through grade-level benchmarks.
  • Student Accessibility Tools: Use read-aloud, highlighting, cross-outs, dark mode, and a dyslexia-friendly font.
  • Progress and Encouragement: Question navigation, progress indicators, and celebrations help students stay engaged.

Teacher Dashboard

Launch sessions fast. Monitor progress live. Use data immediately.

  • Dedicated Grade-Level Dashboards: Use separate Grade 6, Grade 7, and Grade 8 systems with content and reporting aligned to each grade.
  • Create Practice Sessions: Select a support level and assign a full 25-question set or a shorter 10-question mini set.
  • Choose the Reading Level: Each grade includes Below Level, On Level, and Above Level options with grade-appropriate text complexity.
  • Launch Benchmark Assessments: Measure performance using grade-specific standards-based benchmark sessions.
  • Generate Secure Links: Share student links and co-teacher links directly from the dashboard.
  • Flexible Student Sign-In: Students may join with Google sign-in or simple name entry.
  • Monitor Progress Live: See answered questions, correct and incorrect responses, completion status, and question types in real time.
  • Review Class Performance: View overall scores, score bands, question-type accuracy, skill accuracy, and standards performance.
  • Identify Instructional Priorities: See the class’s strongest standards and highest-priority standards after benchmark sessions.
  • View Individual Strengths and Needs: Open a student’s full attempt, question-by-question responses, and student-friendly skill feedback.
  • Track Growth Over Time: Compare student performance across practice and benchmark sessions.
  • Use Session History: Rename, organize, revisit, and compare previous sessions.
  • Export Reports: Download PDF or CSV data for documentation, conferences, intervention planning, and instructional decisions.

Georgia Standards Alignment for Grades 6–8

Each grade-level trainer uses its own Georgia ELA standards catalog, grade-appropriate passages, student-friendly skill descriptions, and standards-based benchmark reporting. Teachers see official standards language while students receive clear explanations of the skills they demonstrated or still need to strengthen.

Standards-based practice and reporting include:

  • Reading Analysis: Text evidence, inference, central idea, theme, summary, and development of ideas.
  • Structure and Craft: Text structure, author’s choices, point of view, perspective, purpose, and word meaning.
  • Source Integration: Paired passages, charts, images, audio, video, and information presented across multiple formats.
  • Argument and Reasoning: Claims, evidence, reasoning, relevance, credibility, and evaluation of support.
  • Language and Vocabulary: Academic vocabulary, context clues, figurative language, grammar, usage, and conventions.
  • Student-Friendly Feedback: Students receive clear strengths and priority areas connected to the skills they practiced.
  • Teacher Standards Reporting: Benchmark dashboards identify strongest standards, priority standards, and individual student performance.
