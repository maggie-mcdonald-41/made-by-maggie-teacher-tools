CER Writing Response Builder

Help students move from a question to a clear, evidence-based response with structured support at every step. The CER Builder guides students through Claim, Evidence, and Reasoning while keeping the thinking—and the writing—in the student’s hands.

Built for real classroom use, the organizer includes a writing coach, sentence starters, auto-fill carry-forward, read-aloud support, dyslexia-friendly formatting, save-and-resume tools, and teacher writing insights for a more transparent writing process.

🧩 Break CER Into Manageable Steps

Instead of asking students to “write a CER response” and hoping they know what to do, the organizer makes each part visible, explicit, and connected.

C

Claim: Restate + Answer

Students see the original prompt, identify the important ideas, restate the question as a statement, and add a clear answer or conclusion.

E

Evidence: Prove It

Students add relevant support from the source. The Evidence coach checks for quotation marks, missing quote pairs, source language, punctuation, and other common writing issues.

R

Reasoning: Explain Why

Students see their Claim and Evidence carried forward, then explain how the evidence supports the claim instead of simply repeating it.

The key difference: students do not have to keep scrolling back, remembering what they wrote, or copying pieces from one section to another. Their own work carries forward so they can focus on the next thinking step.

🎒 Built-In Support Without Doing the Writing

The CER Builder scaffolds the process while preserving student ownership. Students receive reminders, examples, and feedback—but the tool does not generate their response for them.

🧑‍💻 Writing Coach

Students can check each section for common teacher-style concerns, including punctuation, capitalization, informal first- and second-person language outside quotations, contractions, repeated words, casual wording, possible run-ons, and weak or repetitive reasoning.

💬 Evidence Checks

The Evidence section specifically checks for quotation marks and incomplete quote pairs so students can catch missing opening or closing quotation marks before submitting.

🔁 Auto-Fill Carry Forward

The student’s Claim appears while writing Evidence. Claim + Evidence appear while writing Reasoning. The final CER response is assembled automatically from the student’s own writing.

💡 Sentence Starters

Click-to-insert beginnings give students a place to start without supplying the content of the answer. Students still have to finish the thought themselves.

📚 Examples & Teacher Tips

Each section includes a concrete model so students can see what the skill looks like before applying it to their own prompt and source.

📝 Final Response + Checklist

Claim, Evidence, and Reasoning are combined into a final editable response. Students can revise the combined draft and run a final checklist before exporting.

🎧 Read-Aloud Support

Students can listen to individual sections or their final response using the built-in browser reader and selectable voice options.

🔤 Dyslexia-Friendly Mode

A dedicated font toggle and dark-mode option help students adjust the workspace for readability and comfort.

💾 Save, Resume & Export

Local autosave helps protect student work automatically. Optional Google sign-in supports Google Drive saving and Google Docs export for multi-day writing.

🚫 This Is Not AI Writing

The CER Builder is a teaching and scaffolding tool. It does not generate claims, evidence, reasoning, or completed responses for students.

The Writing Coach points students toward issues to revisit, such as missing punctuation, incomplete quotation marks, informal academic language, or reasoning that repeats the evidence. The student must decide how to revise the writing.

The goal is to make the thinking process clearer so students can become more independent writers over time.

👩‍🏫 A Better View of the Writing Process

When a student exports the CER response to Google Docs, the document can include a Teacher Writing Insights section beneath the student’s finished work.

⏱️ Time & Sessions

  • active writing time
  • total elapsed time
  • time spent in each section
  • number of page sessions
  • whether saved work was restored

📋 Paste Activity

  • number of paste events
  • section where each paste occurred
  • pasted character and word counts
  • approximate pasted amount compared with the final response

🛠️ Student Tool Use

  • Writing Coach checks
  • sentence starter use
  • read-aloud use
  • checklist runs
  • final-response editing
  • copy/export actions
Important: The organizer records browser paste events; it does not determine where pasted text came from and does not claim to detect AI use. The activity information gives teachers context about the writing process rather than making unsupported accusations.

🌎 CER Beyond One Subject

Because the organizer separates the writing process from the content, teachers can use it with many types of questions, texts, sources, and investigations.

📖 ELA

Text-dependent responses, literary analysis, paired passages, character analysis, theme, author’s purpose, and short constructed responses.

🔬 Science

Lab conclusions, scientific explanations, observations, data-based claims, phenomenon analysis, and evidence from investigations.

🏛️ Social Studies

Primary and secondary source analysis, historical claims, cause-and-effect explanations, document-based responses, and evidence-based conclusions.

Especially useful for: inclusion/resource classes, writing intervention, test preparation, independent practice, small groups, and classrooms where students need the writing process made more visible and manageable.
